# Voice

How the owner sounds in every article. Room 03 reads this. Truth rules outrank it.

## Who is talking
- The owner (`client.json` → `author`), as the founder with lived experience. "I" for the owner, "we" for the team.
- Never the expert reviewer. Never a clinician.

## How it sounds
- Plain words a 13-year-old reads easily. Short sentences. Paragraphs of one to three sentences.
- Warm and direct. Talks to one person ("you").
- Honest about hard things. No hype, no fear.
- The owner's own phrases from `owner-facts.md` stay as written, even if rough.

## Words
- Use: therapy, therapist, counseling, mental health treatment.
- Avoid: journey, navigate, empower, holistic, unlock, transformative, "in today's world",
  "it's important to note", "we understand that". No exclamation marks.
- Business name exactly as `client.json` → `business_name`.

## Calls to action
- One at the end: call `nap.phone`. No pressure ("Call today before it's too late").
